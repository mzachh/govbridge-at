# Synthetic fixture provenance

All twenty records in `claims.ts` were independently authored for GovBridge AT Demo. They are not exports, paraphrases, pseudonymized copies, or renamed records from an account. Only documented field names, formatting and status-group contracts inspired their structure. The names, dates, amounts and outcomes are invented and are not tariff or entitlement examples.

Reference date: 2026-09-02. Fixed range: 2021-09-03 through 2026-09-02. Four records occupy each rolling twelve-month bucket. The final two records intentionally look identical while retaining distinct fixture keys. No real person, claim identifier, address, bank account, diagnosis or document is included.

Natural-looking practice names are fictional; any resemblance to actual providers is coincidental. Treatment periods and reimbursement dates are deterministic invented examples, not account observations. The user-requested person `Peter`, social security number `1234010196` and invalid placeholder bank account `AT00 1234 1234 1234 1234` are display fixtures, not verified identities. These fields never enter the extension's claim responses. References retain the `SYNTHETIC-demo-claim-*` marker; page-level fictional-data disclosure remains visible.
